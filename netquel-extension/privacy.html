PRIVACY POLICY

Netquel Ship Manager · Last updated: 2025 · Made by Relied
We do not collect your data. Period.

1. Data We Collect

The short answer: nothing leaves your device.

The extension stores the following locally using Chrome's built-in storage API:

• Your Netquel username (for labeling exports)

• Your saved ship names and codes

• Your folder organization

• Your custom command preferences

• Your optional profile photo (stored as base64 locally)

This data never leaves your device. It is never transmitted to Relied, any server, or any third party.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

🚫 We do not collect your Netquel credentials.

🚫 We do not collect your browsing history.

🚫 We do not use analytics, cookies, or tracking pixels.

🚫 We do not transmit any data to external servers.

🚫 We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone.

3. Where Your Data Is Stored

All data is stored using Chrome's chrome.storage.local API, exclusively on your device. Uninstalling the extension deletes this data. You can export a backup JSON at any time from the Settings panel.

4. Network Access

The extension only communicates with Netquel.com — by automating chat messages on that site. No data is sent to Relied or anyone else.

5. Permissions Explained

storage — Save ships and settings locally on your device.

activeTab — Interact with the Netquel.com tab when you press LOAD.

scripting — Inject the content script into Netquel.com pages.

tabs — Open the full-page view and legal pages.

host_permissions (*://*.netquel.com/*) — Extension only runs on Netquel.com. No access to any other website.
